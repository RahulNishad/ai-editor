**14 scenes · 617 words · about 4 minutes at 140 words per minute**

## How to record

- **Format:** Phone vertical 9:16, 1080x1920 or 4K, 30 fps, eye level on tripod, head-to-chest framing. Record in the evening with a few real diyas lit beside you if you can; warm light on your face looks right for this topic.
- **Files:** One clip per scene, named by scene number: 01.mp4, 02.mp4 ... A retake of scene 05 is 05_2.mp4; the highest number wins.
- **Silence:** Stay silent for 2 seconds before and after each line. The pipeline trims it.
- **Shots:** Shot A = medium (chest up). Shot B = close-up (face fills the frame). Switch only where the script says.
- **Expression:** Look into the lens, not at the screen. This is a warm topic: smile when the script says so, stay curious and calm in the history parts. Wear something festive but plain (no logos, no shiny fabric).
- **Every scene needs a clip**, even full-screen ones, because your voice runs under the graphic. For full-screen scenes the camera framing does not matter.
- **Upload** the clips in one folder or zip. Keep your original files; nothing is overwritten.
