# Achha insaan? Panchkula ke baad har maa-baap ke liye paanch sawaal

_Ek aath saal ka bachcha, ek chhe saal ki bachchi, ek band kamra. Jo hua, sirf reports ke mutabik. Aur phir woh sawaal jo yeh ghatna hum sabse poochti hai: hum bachchon ko doctor, engineer bana rahe hain, lekin achha insaan kaun bana raha hai?_

**24 scenes · 1193 words · about 9 minutes at 140 words per minute**

## How to record

- **Format:** Phone vertical 9:16, 1080x1920 or 4K, 30 fps, eye level on tripod, head-to-chest framing. Plain dark or neutral shirt, calm background, soft front light. This is a sensitive topic: no festive colours, no props.
- **Files:** One clip per scene, named by scene number: 01.mp4, 02.mp4 ... 24.mp4. A retake of scene 05 is 05_2.mp4; the highest number wins.
- **Silence:** Stay silent for 2 seconds before and after each line. The pipeline trims it.
- **Shots:** Shot A = medium (chest up). Shot B = close-up (face fills the frame): hook, balance note and sign-off.
- **Expression:** Look into the lens. No smile anywhere before scene 23 except the two small ones the Face notes allow (scenes 9 and 15). Never show anger at the child, the family or the school; the tone is a worried parent, not a prosecutor. Each scene's last line hands over to the next scene: say it as a bridge, do not drop your energy before the cut.
- **Transitions:** The scenes are written as one continuous talk. Where a line ends with a question or 'aankde dekhiye' / 'yahi chautha sawaal hai', hold your eyes on the lens for a beat after it; the next clip answers it.
- **Every scene needs a clip**, even full-screen ones, because your voice runs under the graphic. For full-screen scenes the camera framing does not matter.
- **Upload** the clips in one folder or zip. Keep your original files; nothing is overwritten.
