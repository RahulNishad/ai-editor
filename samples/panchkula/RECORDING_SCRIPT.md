# Achha insaan kaun bana raha hai? Panchkula ke baad har maa-baap ke paanch sawaal

_Ek aath saal ka bachcha, ek chhe saal ki bachchi, ek band kamra: jo hua, sirf reports ke mutabik. Aur phir woh paanch sawaal jo yeh kahani hum sab maa-baap se poochti hai._

**24 scenes · 1256 words · about 9 minutes at 140 words per minute**

## How to record

- **Format:** Phone vertical 9:16, 1080x1920 or 4K, 30 fps, eye level on tripod, head-to-chest framing. Plain dark or neutral shirt, calm background, soft front light. This is a sensitive topic: no festive colours, no props.
- **Files:** One clip per scene, named by scene number: 01.mp4, 02.mp4 ... 24.mp4. A retake of scene 05 is 05_2.mp4; the highest number wins.
- **Silence:** Stay silent for 2 seconds before and after each line. The pipeline trims it.
- **Shots:** Shot A = medium (chest up). Shot B = close-up (face fills the frame): hook, balance note and sign-off.
- **Expression:** This is you telling a story to one person, not reading news. Look into the lens the whole time. Speak at talking pace, a little slower than usual; the pauses marked with '…' are real pauses. No smile before scene 23 except the two small ones the Face notes allow (scenes 9 and 15). Never show anger at the child, the family or the school: the voice is a worried parent thinking aloud, not a prosecutor. Each scene's last line is a bridge into the next clip: say it to the lens, hold your eyes there for a beat, and only then stop.
- **Transitions:** Every clip's last line hands over to the next clip (a question, a 'lekin', a chapter title). Keep the same energy across the cut: no new 'toh', no breath, no re-greeting. You are telling one story, not reading 24 cards.
- **Every scene needs a clip**, even full-screen ones, because your voice runs under the graphic. For full-screen scenes the camera framing does not matter.
- **Upload** the clips in one folder or zip. Keep your original files; nothing is overwritten.
