# Achha insaan kaun banayega? (Short)

_Independent vertical Short (~100-110 s as written; a 75-s cut drops the NCERT and JJB sentences), recorded separately from the long video. One idea: Panchkula ke baad har maa-baap ka ek sawaal, told through two 'aaine' (ghar, school). Facts and attributions are the long script's; nothing new is claimed here._

**7 scenes · 270 words · about 2 minutes at 140 words per minute**

## How to record

- **Format:** Phone vertical 9:16, 1080x1920 or 4K, 30 fps, eye level on tripod, head-to-chest framing, green cloth filling the frame. Same dark or neutral shirt as the long video. Sensitive topic: no festive colours, no props.
- **Files:** One clip per scene in a separate folder (panchkula-short): 01.mp4 ... 07.mp4. A retake of scene 03 is 03_2.mp4; the highest number wins. Record every scene, including the two voice-only fact scenes (04, 05).
- **Silence:** Stay silent for 2 seconds before and after each line. The pipeline trims it.
- **Shots:** Shot B (close-up, face fills the frame) for scenes 01, 03 and 07; Shot A (chest up) for the rest. In a Short your face is the thumbnail: close and steady.
- **Expression:** Faster than the long video but never hurried: a Short is one thought said to one person. Look into the lens the whole time. No smile until the sign-off. No anger at the child, the family or the school: you are a worried parent thinking aloud. Each clip's last line hands over to the next one; hold your eyes on the lens for a beat after it.
- **Transitions:** No 'doston', no channel intro, no 'is video mein jaaniye'. The first line is the hook. Keep one energy across the seven clips: no new 'toh', no breath, no re-greeting.
- **Every scene needs a clip**, even full-screen ones, because your voice runs under the graphic. For full-screen scenes the camera framing does not matter.
- **Upload** the clips in one folder or zip. Keep your original files; nothing is overwritten.
