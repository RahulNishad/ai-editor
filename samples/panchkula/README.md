# Sample: Panchkula — "Achha insaan?"

An 8-minute investigative documentary for Think With Rahul Nishad on the Panchkula school assault case (28 October 2026) and the bigger question it raises about parenting and emotional education. Built with the same pipeline as `samples/fz1073` and `samples/diwali` (the Python, JS and HTML files are copies; `node_modules` and `.venv` are symlinks to the fz1073 ones).

Written on 8 November 2026 in a background session. The script is `script.long.json`; `RECORDING_SCRIPT.md` and `STORYBOARD.jpg` are generated from it.

## Hard rules for this topic (apply to every cut, Short, thumbnail and comment)

- Neither child, nor either family, is named, shown or hinted at. The boy is "the accused child" / "aath saal ka bachcha"; the girl is "chhe saal ki bachchi". The Juvenile Justice Board has ordered the removal of videos and photographs of the incident; Section 74 of the JJ Act 2015 bars disclosing a child's identity. No CCTV frames, no viral video, no homecoming photos, no photos of the school (its name is also not read out in full), the parents, the teachers or the hospital ward.
- No reconstruction of the attack. No blood, no weapons, no faces in AI images. AI images are generic and labelled "AI ILLUSTRATION".
- Causes are questions or attributed expert views, never conclusions about this child. The "Free Fire / PUBG" angle is reported only as "jaanch ka ek hissa" (a line of inquiry), because that is all the sources say.
- Every claim is attributed ("Tribune ke mutabik", "parivaar ka aarop hai", "JJB ne kaha"). The school's side is not reported anywhere; the script says so.

## Storytelling pass (9 Nov 2026)

Rahul asked for the script to sound like him telling a story, not reading news. The rewrite in `script.long.json` keeps every scene, layout, card, photo, source line and number from the first version and changes only `text`, `text_dev`, `direction`, `expression` and the key phrases. What changed in the voice:

- First person throughout ("ek sawaal main aaj khud se pooch raha hoon", "main na dikhaunga", "mera sabse bada sawaal"), short sentences, rhetorical questions, one relatable "sochiye" picture per chapter (the tired father, the evening question "test mein kitne aaye?").
- Structure: hook (the parents' dream vs "achha insaan kaun banayega?") → the promise and the scene (0:27) → the story in order (the room, the hospital, the FIR against the school, the Board, the law) → the pivot "is khabar ne mujhse paanch sawaal pooche" (2:50) → five chapters where each number is told as part of the story, not read off a card → the human angle and fairness scene (7:45) → the conclusion that loops back to the hook → the comment question → sign-off.
- A new question or development every 20–30 s; every scene's last line is a spoken bridge into the next clip ("Chaliye, us dopahar chalte hain", "Lekin pehle kanoon suniye", "Yahi mera chautha sawaal hai", "Paanch baatein", "Lekin ek baat aur").
- Facts, allegations and opinions stay marked in the voice: "Tribune ne likha", "parivaar ka aarop hai", "reports ke mutabik", "yeh main nahi jaanta", "jaanch ka hissa hai, nateeja nahi". No scene, conversation or motive is invented; the only quoted words are the family's allegation already in the reports and the expert advice line.
- Length: 1,256 words by `script_to_md.py` (9.0 min at 140 wpm; the storyboard's padded estimate is 9.4 min). That is slightly above the 1,100–1,200 target; the easiest 60-word trims if Rahul wants them are the gali line in scene 13 and the MWCD 2007 sentence in scene 10 (the card keeps the number).
- Two small corrections made while rewriting: the scene 3 card said "Tuesday"; 28 Oct 2026 is a Wednesday (computed, not from a source), so the card now says Wednesday. Scene 6 now says the parents gave a written undertaking of counselling, which matches the reports (not a Board order).
- Flagged, not changed: everything in "Before publishing" below still applies (India Today / Indian Express pages, NCERT PDF, any school statement).
