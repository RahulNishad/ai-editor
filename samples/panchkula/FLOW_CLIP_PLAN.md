# Panchkula video: animation plan (for approval; nothing generated, the gate applies)

Per the Flow validation gate in `CLAUDE.md`, nothing goes to Google Flow (or any paid image/video tool) until two independent reviewer agents say READY and Rahul approves the prompts and the start stills. The script already works with real Commons photos and code cards in every scene; these four clips would replace a photo where a moving generic image says more than a still.

Rules for this topic: no faces (no real people, no invented child faces either), no school signboard, no weapon, no blood, no uniform or badge that could point at a real school, no readable text, no brand logos, no game interface that names a real game. Every clip is labelled "AI ILLUSTRATION". Nothing may look like a reconstruction of the attack.

## Also considered and dropped

- Any image of a child alone in a locked room, a brick, or a corridor with a child: these are reconstructions of the attack and are banned for this topic.
- A school gate or signboard: even an invented one would be read as the real school.
- A "boy playing a violent game" image: it would turn a line of inquiry into a verdict.
